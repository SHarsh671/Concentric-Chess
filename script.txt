As you all know, traditional chess has been played on a square board for more than a thousand years, with thousands of variants created over the centuries. Today we introduce two new variants developed by Dr. Rapoport, concentric and archimedean chess.

[Scroll homepage to the two variants]
Concentric chess uses a board formed of 6 concentric rings totaling 72 cells, while archimedean chess uses two interlocking archimedean spirals. Both variants use the same pieces as traditional chess, a new piece the prince, and some updated move mechanics to match the new board structures.

Now what makes these variants unique?

XThe prince meanwhile is a new piece that can move like a king but is immune to check.
